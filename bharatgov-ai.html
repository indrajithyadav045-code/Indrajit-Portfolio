01 / Overview

The idea behind BharatGov AI.

BharatGov AI is intended to reduce the effort involved in discovering relevant government schemes and services. The project focuses on a more understandable interface for navigating information that can otherwise feel fragmented.

The repository describes an AI-assisted experience for natural-language discovery, multilingual queries and guided service workflows. Users should confirm eligibility and final requirements against official government sources.

Project note
Government scheme rules and eligibility can change. Treat AI responses as guidance and verify important details on the relevant official portal.
